SUBJECT: Refund Processing Time Advisory

Every approved refund must be accompanied by an advisory that settlement is not immediate.

Funds are returned to the original method of payment and typically clear within 10 business days, depending on the processing times of the customer's bank or card issuer.

This advisory applies only once a refund has been approved. It sets no condition on eligibility and is never a reason to deny a request. The period stated here describes how long settlement takes and has nothing to do with any refund window.
