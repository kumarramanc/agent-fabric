EFFECTIVE DATE: 2023-02-15
SUBJECT: Duplicate Refund Prevention Policy

An order cannot be refunded more than once.

To apply this policy, review the refund_status of the order before processing any refund. Refunding an order returns the money and voids the tickets, so the action is final and can be neither reversed nor repeated.

If refund_status is false, the order has not yet been refunded and may be refunded, provided no other policy prevents it. If refund_status is true, the request must be denied, and the customer should be informed that the order was already refunded on the date recorded in refund_date.
